# Cactus module card art - image generator prompts

32 images, one per module. All share one style block so the set reads as a family.

## Spec

- **Aspect ratio: 16:9.** Generate at 1536x864 (or 1920x1080 and downscale).
- **Export:** WebP, quality 80-85, resized to **1200x675**. Aim under ~90 KB each.
- **No transparency.** The background must be painted edge to edge. A transparent PNG will
  look broken on one theme or the other.
- **Light and dark mode:** the deep forest-green background is darker than the light-mode
  card (`#ffffff`) and greener/lighter than the dark-mode card (`#1c1a17`), so one file
  separates from the card on both themes. Do not generate light-background variants.
- **Filenames:** exactly the slug listed with each prompt, lowercase, `.webp`.
- **Drop them in:** `public/module-art/`

## The style block (identical in every prompt below - do not vary it)

> 3D render, soft matte clay / plasticine materials, rounded edges, no sharp corners, no
> glossy highlights. A single object, centred, three-quarter view from slightly above,
> occupying about 55% of the frame height with generous even margins on all sides.
> Background: smooth deep forest-green gradient running from #1a4c3a in the top-left to
> #0e2a1e in the bottom-right, edge to edge, fully opaque, no transparency, no alpha
> channel. Soft warm key light from the upper left, cool green fill from the lower right,
> one soft contact shadow under the object. Colour palette strictly limited to forest
> greens #2c7558 #1a4c3a #133929, warm cream #faf9f7, pale sand #d2cdbf, and a single
> terracotta accent #e05232 used on one focal detail only. Faint soft radial glow behind
> the object. Very fine film grain over the whole image. No text, no letters, no numbers,
> no logos, no watermarks, no user interfaces, no screenshots, no people, no hands, no
> brand marks. Aspect ratio 16:9.
